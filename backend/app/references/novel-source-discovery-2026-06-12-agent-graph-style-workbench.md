## Absorbed rules

- Skill/agent workbenches can provide role vocabulary and review structure, but
  upstream skill bodies, agents, prompts, scripts, source material, and example
  worlds stay outside project context.
- Long-form generation should expose a node graph: template version, Lore tree,
  downstream OUTDATED nodes, and fix-loop severity thresholds must be visible
  before generated chapters are treated as current.
- Uploaded TXT style-learning requires rights/consent, source scope, encoding
  notes, provider/API custody, and a style-feature abstraction step. It must not
  become named-author style cloning.
- Editorial agent flows should bind Writer, Reviewer, and Editor roles to story
  memory, timeline facts, CharacterGraph edges, and style cards. Auto-injected
  facts stay advisory until conflict review confirms them.
