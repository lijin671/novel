## Reusable pattern

The durable pattern is that a continuation/same-type writing task must expose
its creative contract before prose:

- selected mode stays explicit: `continue-chapter`, `full-project`, `revise`,
  `analyze`, `export`, or `quick-start`
- chapter contract requires job, reader promise, opening hook, goal, obstacle,
  payoff, new hook, and forbidden contradiction
- scene beat sheet should exist before drafting; most chapters use 3-7 scenes
  with changed exit state
- reader promise and chapter-level micro-payoff are first-class gates
- accepted chapter write-back includes measurable length, not just summary
- revision order must fix structure, character, continuity, and scene purpose
  before line polish
